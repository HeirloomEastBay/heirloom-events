Get to know the farm

Heirloom is an intentional Christian community, a church, a retreat center and a small sustainable farm.

Heirloom is located on Lisjan Ohlone land. We pay a Shuumi Land Tax, a voluntary annual contribution that supports the work of the Sogorea Te’ Land Trust.

Heirloom is a ministry of the NorCal District of the Church of the Nazarene.
